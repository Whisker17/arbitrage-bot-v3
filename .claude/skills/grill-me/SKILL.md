---
name: grill-me
description: A relentless interview to sharpen a plan or design — goal, constraints and acceptance — one question at a time.
disable-model-invocation: true
---

Interview me relentlessly about every aspect of this plan until we reach a shared understanding. Walk down each branch of the decision tree, resolving dependencies between decisions one by one. For each question, give your recommended answer.

Ask one question at a time and wait for my answer before continuing. Several questions at once is bewildering.

If a *fact* can be found by exploring the environment (files, docs, tools, the tracker), look it up instead of asking. The *decisions* are mine, so put each one to me and wait. Do not re-ask anything already settled in the conversation, the spec, or `docs/DESIGN.md`.

Aim for a clear goal, the constraints, the dependencies and checkable acceptance. Do not act on the plan until I confirm we have reached a shared understanding. `/to-spec` writes it down after that.
